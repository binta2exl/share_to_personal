UNDERWRITING ENGINE

15 Deterministic Underwriting Modifiers (CNA Matrix)

Raw profile facts are programmatically mapped to the 15 standard CNA cyber underwriting risk dimensions.

1. CORPORATE SCALE
  • Years in Business: Resilience & longevity (>10 yrs = Favourable)
  • Geographic Spread: Multi-state/global regulatory footprint
  • Organizational Complexity: Tiered subsidiary hierarchy
  • M&A Activity: Unvetted legacy IT integration risk
2. FINANCIAL STABILITY
  • Seasonality of Sales: Revenue spike windows (Q4 ransomware risk)
  • Volatility in Sales: Erratic YoY cashflow recovery resilience
  • Nature of Services: SEC SIC industry hazard classification
3. TECHNICAL SURFACE
  • Domain Encryption: TLS cipher grade & HTTPS enforcement
  • Internet Footprint: Discovered domains & public assets
  • B2C End Products: Payment gateway & PCI-DSS attack vector
  • Cyber Security: Public SOC2 / ISO compliance
4. COMPLIANCE & BREACH
  • Sensitive Information: PII, PHI, financial consumer data
  • Privacy Regulation: Statutory GDPR / CCPA liability
  • Breach History: Past ransomware & FTC disclosures
  • Active Litigation: Ongoing data disputes in courts
DETERMINISTIC SCORING FORMULA: Evidence → Modifier Rules Engine → Category Score (1.0 to 6.0) → Final Rating Modifier
• Very Favourable (1.0 - 2.0 / Credit)  |  • Favourable (2.1 - 3.5)  |  • Average / Neutral (3.6 - 4.5)  |  • Unfavourable (4.6 - 6.0 / Debit)
