The Problem:
- Commercial policies (General Liability, Auto, Workers' Comp, Property) are static and reviewed once a year, while businesses evolve constantly (e.g., adding delivery fleets, expanding premises, or hiring in new states). 
- When unlisted risks trigger losses, claims are denied, creating major coverage disputes.

The Solution & Differentiation:
- An autonomous, multi-agent AI engine that monitors external operational triggers (e.g., license filings, web updates, POS data) and cross-audits them across multiple LOBs simultaneously. 
- It flags exposure gaps and drafts needed policy endorsements with explainable citations.

Business Value:
 * For Carriers: Captures mid-term endorsement premium immediately (rather than waiting for renewals), cuts costly claim-dispute litigation, and smooths annual audit friction.
 * For Businesses: Prevents catastrophic out-of-pocket claim denials and eliminates surprise year-end audit adjustments.
 * For Brokers: Eliminates manual renewal discovery calls and automates cross-sell endorsement workflows.
