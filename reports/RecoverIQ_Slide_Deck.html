Problem Statement
Why Does This Matter to Insurers? Reinsurance Leakage Drivers
  • A. Siloed Claims Blindness: Individual losses ($500k–$900k) appear under retention, missing $900k+ aggregate CAT event payouts.
  • B. Treaty Complexity: Multi-layered retentions, hours clauses, and peril exclusions lead to miscalculated entitlements.
  • C. Billing Ledger Gaps: Disconnected claims, billing logs, and broker bordereaux (BDX) hide unbilled & short-paid recoveries.
  • D. Manual Audit Bottleneck: Spreadsheet-based evidence gathering causes costly collection delays and unrecovered balances.
Solution Architecture
How RecoverIQ Solves It: The 4-Pillar Autonomous Engine
  • A. AI Treaty Interpretation: Groq-hosted LLM extracts terms, retentions, and exclusions with verifiable contract page citations.
  • B. Deterministic XOL Math: Aggregate catastrophe losses evaluated against available treaty capacity with 100% bug-free rules.
  • C. 3-Way Reconciliation: Simultaneously matches Entitled Recovery vs Actually Billed vs Broker Remittances (BDX).
  • D. Actionable Audit Evidence: Auto-drafts legal billing demand briefs with step-by-step proofs for 1-click analyst approval.
